# Direction: EnrichedLab provenance console

Register: surface

Work patterns: monitor, operate, explore

## Domain material

- Proof object: a hash-addressed event, its causal edge, and the artifact or decision it produced.
- Made of: a ruled lab record, an operations console, and a provenance ledger.
- Native rhythm: ordered events, lifecycle phases, evidence classes, and decision boundaries.
- Reader: a scientist or operator who returns often and may need to reconstruct one step months later.

## Axes

| Axis | Choice | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Composition | Instrument frame | The run selector, replay position, graph, and record inspector form one workspace. |
| Color | UC Davis signal palette | Blue is structure, gold marks selection or attention, red is reserved for failed or denied state. |
| Type | Humanist sans with mono evidence | Interface copy stays compact; IDs, hashes, queries, and timestamps use monospace. |
| Depth | Recessed canvas | The graph is a work surface. Records use rules and tonal steps instead of floating cards. |
| Edges | Hairline rules | Small radii, clear separators, no colored accent rails. |
| Motion | Instrument | Motion only shows graph navigation, loading, connection state, or a confirmed control change. |
| Texture | Ruled and gridded | Lane bands, time ticks, and evidence rows provide the structure. |

## Outlier

The provenance workspace may be denser than the rest of the product. It should look like a scientific instrument, not a
marketing dashboard. Density is controlled with grouping, search, paging, and a record inspector rather than larger
cards.

## Refused

- Gradients, glass surfaces, neon glow, and decorative blur.
- Colored left rails as a record category marker.
- A detached lane key that becomes wrong after pan or zoom.
- “Time machine” as the operator-facing feature name.
- Manual refresh as the normal live-data path.
- Claims that emitted reasoning is hidden model state or scientific authority.
